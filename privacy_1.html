Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2 October 2026

TankMate is an app for people who share a car. It helps them book the car, log journeys and split fuel costs fairly. This policy explains what information the app uses and where it goes. The short version: TankMate keeps your data on your own device. It has no accounts, no analytics, no advertising and no third-party tracking, and the developer does not collect or see the information you enter.

1. Information stored on your device

Everything you enter, such as bookings, journeys, fill-ups, car details, receipt or odometer photos, and the names of the people you share the car with, is stored only on your device. TankMate has no accounts or sign-in, and the developer does not run any server that receives this information. Names you add for other people are just text you type in; they are not accounts.

If you delete the app, this information is deleted with it, unless it is included in a device backup (see section 9).

2. Location

If you allow it, TankMate uses your device's location while the app is open to set a journey's starting point and to help find places. Location access is optional and controlled by iOS: you can refuse it or change it at any time in Settings, and you can still enter journeys by hand. TankMate keeps the resulting addresses and distances on your device only. It does not save your current position: that is held only while the app is open and is forgotten when the app closes.

3. Apple Maps

Place search, address suggestions, route and mileage calculation, maps, and turning your location into a readable address are provided by Apple Maps. To do this, the addresses you type, your approximate location, and the start and end points of a journey are sent to Apple. This is handled by Apple under Apple's privacy policy. TankMate does not send this information anywhere else.

4. Fuel prices

TankMate shows nearby fuel prices using the UK government's Fuel Finder data. The app downloads the national price list through a small server run by the developer, which passes the request on to the government service. The app sends only a page number, the time of its last update, and a key identifying the app. It sends nothing about you, your location, your car, or your bookings and journeys. The comparison of "cheapest near me" is done on your device, and the app fetches prices at most once a day.

As with any internet request, the hosting provider for this small server (Cloudflare) may see technical details such as your device's IP address when the request is made. We do not use this to identify or track you. To prevent misuse, the server limits how many requests it accepts from one internet address in a short period. It does not log or store your requests.

5. Notifications

If you allow them, TankMate sends reminders such as MOT, insurance, service and fill-up reminders. These are scheduled on your device by the app itself, and no notification data leaves your device. You can turn them off in iOS Settings.

6. Calendar

If you choose to export a booking, TankMate adds it to your calendar. It has write-only access, so it can add events but cannot read your existing ones. The event includes the person's name, the car, the times and the planned destination. If your calendar syncs to iCloud, Google or another service, that service will hold the event under its own policy.

7. Photos

If you attach a receipt or odometer photo, TankMate uses the iOS photo picker, which gives the app only the photo you choose. It does not have access to the rest of your photo library. The photo is stored on your device.

8. Home Screen widget

If you add the TankMate widget, it shows balances and upcoming bookings on your Home Screen. To do this, the app saves a small summary (names, balances and the next few bookings) in a storage area on your device that only TankMate and its widget can read. Nothing is sent off your device. Anyone who can see your Home Screen can see the widget; amounts are hidden where iOS hides widget content on a locked device. You can remove the widget at any time.

9. Sharing, exporting and backups

TankMate sends your information nowhere by itself. If you tap Share on a trip card or monthly summary, it is created on your device and passed to whichever app or person you choose, and what happens next is up to you.

You can also create a backup file of your TankMate data from Settings. It contains your personal data, including place names and addresses (and route maps if you choose to include them). It is created on your device and only goes where you choose to send it.

Your device's normal iCloud or computer backup may include TankMate's data, depending on your own iOS settings.

10. Third parties

We do not sell, rent or share your information. TankMate contains no third-party code for analytics, crash reporting or advertising. The only outside services involved are Apple (Maps, iOS, and your calendar or backups if you use them) and the UK government fuel price data described above.

If you have chosen in iOS Settings to share analytics with app developers, Apple may give the developer anonymous crash and usage statistics. These never include the information you enter in TankMate.

11. Children

TankMate is not aimed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect information from anyone.

12. Your rights

Because your data stays on your device, you have full control: you can view, edit and delete it inside the app, or delete everything by removing the app. If you have questions about your rights under UK GDPR, or anything in this policy, contact us using the details below.

13. Changes

TankMate is still being developed. If future features (such as syncing between devices, signing in, or looking up a car by registration number) change what information is handled, this policy will be updated before they are released and the "Last updated" date will change.
